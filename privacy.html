Privacy Policy

PAST MIDNIGHT UPLOADER · LAST UPDATED: JULY 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the application Past Midnight Uploader ("the App") handles information. The App is a personal, single-user tool operated by the owner of the Past Midnight YouTube channel to publish videos to that same channel.

Who this App is for

The App is used solely by its developer and channel owner to upload their own video content to their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to, installed by, or made available to other users, and it does not process data belonging to anyone other than the owner.

What the App accesses

Through Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization, the App requests the following scopes on the owner's Google Account:

  • youtube.upload — to upload video files to the owner's channel.
  • youtube — to set the title, description, category, thumbnail, and privacy status of those uploaded videos.

The App uses these permissions only to publish the owner's own videos. It does not read, view, collect, or share any other account data, viewer data, analytics, comments, or personal information.

How data is stored

The App runs locally on the owner's own computer. The OAuth authorization token is stored only on that local machine and is used to authenticate upload requests to YouTube. No credentials or tokens are transmitted to, or stored on, any third-party server operated by the App. The App has no database, no analytics, and no backend service.

Data sharing

The App does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties. The only external service it communicates with is the official YouTube Data API, for the sole purpose of uploading the owner's videos.

Google API Services / Limited Use

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the App's single function — uploading the owner's videos to the owner's channel — and is not used for any other purpose.

Data retention and deletion

The only stored item is the local OAuth token, which the owner can delete at any time by removing the token file from their computer or by revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
