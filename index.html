About

Past Midnight is a YouTube channel of calm, cinematic documentaries about the deep history of everything — impossible stars, vanished civilizations, the nights that made us. Slow enough to fall asleep to, strange enough to keep you up.

A new journey into the past, every night — just past midnight.

Past Midnight Uploader

Past Midnight Uploader is the private tool the channel owner uses to publish new episodes to their own YouTube channel. It connects to the owner's YouTube account through Google's official API, uploads the finished video, and sets its title, description, and thumbnail — the same steps a person would do by hand in YouTube Studio, automated for a daily publishing schedule.

The tool is used only by the channel owner, for their own channel. It does not access, collect, or process data from any other user.
